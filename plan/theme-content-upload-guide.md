# Theme Content Upload Guide

This guide lists **every piece of content** the Tea Garden Eco Resort theme uses from the dashboard.  
Upload these yourself at any time via **Dashboard → Website** (or Rooms/Menu pages).

## Section-by-Section Details

---

### 1. Logo
**Where:** Dashboard → Website → Hero & About → Logo URL  
**What to upload:** A transparent `.png` file with your resort logo.  
**Recommended size:** At least 200px wide, transparent background.  
**If not uploaded:** The theme shows 🌿 + your resort name in serif font (looks fine as a fallback).

---

### 2. Hero Background Image
**Where:** Dashboard → Website → Hero & About → Hero Image  
**What to upload:** A wide landscape photo — tea garden, misty hills, or nature scene.  
**Recommended size:** **1920 × 1080px minimum** (full-screen, so bigger = sharper).  
**Tips:**
- Landscape orientation (wider than tall)
- Green/misty/natural tones work best
- The theme adds a dark green overlay so the photo can be slightly bright
- Avoid photos where the horizon line is exactly in the middle

---

### 3. Hero Title
**Where:** Dashboard → Website → Hero & About → Hero Title  
**What to enter:** The main big heading shown over the hero image.  
**Example:** `Where the Mist Meets the Hills`  
**Character limit:** Keep under 60 characters (it wraps at larger sizes).

---

### 4. Hero Subtitle
**Where:** Dashboard → Website → Hero & About → Hero Subtitle  
**What to enter:** A short line shown below the hero title.  
**Example:** `A sanctuary of tea gardens, fresh air, and quiet luxury in the Sylhet highlands.`  
**Character limit:** Keep under 120 characters.

---

### 5. About Section Image
**Where:** Dashboard → Website → Hero & About → About Image  
**What to upload:** A scenic photo — garden view, eco cottage exterior, nature path, or pool.  
**Recommended size:** **800 × 600px or 900 × 700px** (portrait or landscape both work).  
**Tips:**
- The image is shown in a rounded frame on the right side of the About section
- Avoid very dark photos — the frame adds a slight green tint at the bottom

---

### 6. About Title
**Where:** Dashboard → Website → Hero & About → About Title  
**What to enter:** Heading for the About/Our Story section.  
**Example:** `Born from the Heart of the Tea Garden`

---

### 7. About Description Text
**Where:** Dashboard → Website → Hero & About → About Text  
**What to enter:** 2–4 sentences describing your resort's story or philosophy.  
**Example:**  
> Nestled among 50 acres of working tea gardens in the Sylhet hills, our eco resort was built to preserve the land while sharing its beauty with discerning guests. Every cottage is constructed from local timber and bamboo. Every meal is grown on our organic estate.

---

### 8. Gallery Images (6–8 images)
**Where:** Dashboard → Website → Gallery  
**What to upload:** 6 to 8 photos of the resort.

**Recommended photos (in this order for best layout):**
| Slot | Subject | Notes |
|------|---------|-------|
| 1 (featured — largest) | Tea garden panorama or resort exterior | Landscape, 1000px+ wide |
| 2 | Indoor room or suite interior | Any orientation |
| 3 | Garden path or nature walk | Any |
| 4 | Eco cottage or bungalow exterior | Any |
| 5 | Valley / sunrise / misty view | Landscape preferred |
| 6 (wide span) | Restaurant, dining area, or food | Landscape preferred |
| 7 | Tea plucking or garden close-up | Any |
| 8 | Lounge, hammock, or relaxation area | Any |

**Recommended size:** Minimum **800 × 600px** per image. JPG format is fine.  
**Note:** If no gallery images are uploaded, the theme shows placeholder images automatically.

---

### 9. Room Images
**Where:** Dashboard → Rooms → click any room → Edit → Images  
**What to upload:** 2–4 photos per room showing bed, bathroom, view, balcony.  
**Recommended size:** **800 × 600px** (landscape works best for room cards).  
**Why important:** The Rooms section cards show the first uploaded image for each room.

---

### 10. Primary Color
**Where:** Dashboard → Website → SEO & Branding → Primary Color  
**Value to set:** `#1a6b2a`  
This is the **deep forest green** used for buttons, navbar (when scrolled), footer background.

---

### 11. Accent Color
**Where:** Dashboard → Website → SEO & Branding → Accent Color  
**Value to set:** `#d3d558`  
This is the **lime/chartreuse yellow** used for CTA buttons, eyebrow labels, bullet points, quote marks.

---

### 12. Guest Testimonials
**Where:** Dashboard → Website → Testimonials → Add Testimonial  
**What to enter per testimonial:**
- Guest name (e.g., `Reza Ahmed`)
- Review text (1–3 sentences)
- Star rating (1–5)
- Avatar image (optional — small square photo, 100×100px)

**Recommended:** Add 3–6 testimonials for the section to look complete.

---

### 13. WhatsApp Number
**Where:** Dashboard → Website → Social Media → WhatsApp Number  
**Format:** Include country code, no spaces: `+8801712345678`  
**Effect:** Shows a floating green WhatsApp button on the bottom-right of the website.

---

### 14. Social Media Links
**Where:** Dashboard → Website → Social Media  
**Fields available:**
- Facebook URL
- Instagram URL
- Twitter/X URL
- TikTok URL
- YouTube URL
- TripAdvisor URL

These show as icons in the footer.

---

### 15. SEO Title & Description
**Where:** Dashboard → Website → SEO & Branding  
**SEO Title example:** `Sylhet Tea Garden Eco Resort | Nature Retreat`  
**SEO Description example:** `A hilltop eco resort nestled in the tea gardens of Sylhet. Organic dining, eco cottages, and guided garden walks.`  
**Why:** Used in Google search results and social media previews.

---

### 16. Menu Items (Restaurant Menu section)
**Where:** Dashboard → Menu → Add Item  
**Fields per item:**
- Name
- Category (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Appetizer, Dessert, Beverage, Special)
- Price
- Available on/off toggle
- Image (optional)

**Note:** The Menu section is visible on the website only if it is **not hidden** in Dashboard → Website → Sections.

---

### 17. Template Selection
**Where:** Dashboard → Website → Template tab  
**Action:** Click "Tea Garden Eco Resort" card and Save.  
This must be done first — without this, the website shows the default Luxe theme.

---

## What You Do NOT Need to Upload

These things are **automatic** — the theme handles them without any content upload:

- Availability calendar (uses real booking data)
- Booking form (works from room data)
- Room cards layout (generated from Rooms database)
- Sticky navbar scroll behavior (built-in)
- Mobile responsive layout (built-in)
- WhatsApp floating button (shows if number is set)
- Special offers section (shows if any active offers exist)

---

## Minimum Viable Upload (to go live quickly)

If you're in a hurry, upload just these 5 things and the site will look good:

1. **Hero image** — most visible part of the page
2. **Hero title** — big heading on the hero
3. **Gallery images** — at least 4 photos
4. **Room images** — at least 1 photo per room
5. **Template selection** — set to Tea Garden

Everything else can be added gradually.
