# ResortPro social-media context

Use this file whenever writing social posts for ResortPro. The canonical
business context is `FOUNDER_CONTEXT.md`; this file translates it into
platform-native social guidance.

## Account identity

- Brand: ResortPro
- Category: resort management software / hospitality operating system
- Core audience: independent resort owners, general managers, and small resort
  groups, with an initial Bangladesh-relevant lens
- Product role: connect bookings, front desk, payments, guest records,
  housekeeping, restaurant operations, and direct booking in one shared view
- Primary social goal until changed: build category awareness and drive
  qualified owners/managers to the live demo or plan page

## Voice

Write like a calm resort operator who also understands software.

- Warm, observant, and useful
- Confident without exaggeration
- Operationally specific rather than feature-list heavy
- Plainspoken rather than corporate
- Respectful of teams currently using notebooks, WhatsApp, or spreadsheets;
  never mock the workaround that kept their property running
- Hospitality-first: the staff and guest outcome lead, the software follows

## Language

- Facebook and local short-form video: Bangla-first, using familiar English
  hospitality/product terms naturally
- LinkedIn: English-first or bilingual when the topic is Bangladesh-specific
- YouTube: match the spoken language; provide searchable English product terms
  where helpful
- Do not force literal Bangla translations of common terms such as booking,
  check-in, dashboard, room status, payment, CRM, and report
- Avoid mixing languages merely to sound trendy

## Content pillars

### 1. The resort day, made visible

Show one concrete moment: morning arrivals, unpaid balances, room turnover,
walk-in booking, restaurant order, or a returning guest. Explain how shared
information improves the handoff.

### 2. Practical hospitality operations

Teach useful methods even when ResortPro is not mentioned: front-desk
checklists, direct-booking practices, housekeeping coordination, guest-history
hygiene, reporting habits, and payment reconciliation.

### 3. Direct guest relationships

Cover booking pages/widgets, reducing booking friction, guest communication,
offers, loyalty, and how an owned booking journey should reflect the property.
Do not promise “commission-free” without verified commercial details.

### 4. Building ResortPro

Share product decisions, workflow observations, bilingual design choices, and
the reasoning behind features. Keep founder/building posts candid and specific.
Do not invent personal anecdotes.

### 5. Product proof

Use screen recordings, feature walkthroughs, verified customer stories, and
measured outcomes. Until evidence exists, demonstrate capability rather than
claiming business results.

## Platform guidance

### Facebook

- Tone: conversational, practical, Bangla-first
- Best formats: short workflow story, before/after process, checklist, demo clip
- End with a direct question or one CTA
- Links may appear in the post when needed
- Use 0-3 relevant hashtags; do not add generic hashtag blocks

### LinkedIn

- Tone: professional, human, and opinionated with evidence
- Best formats: operational insight, founder/product decision, market lesson,
  verified mini case study
- Keep the first two lines strong and specific
- Put external links in the first comment when reach is the priority
- Use 3-5 focused hashtags

### Instagram

- Visual leads; caption explains one takeaway
- Put the hook inside the first 125 characters
- Best formats: Reel walkthrough, carousel checklist, property workflow visual
- Use 3-10 relevant hashtags and write useful alt text
- Send traffic through link in bio or Stories link sticker

### YouTube / Shorts

- Long-form: keyword-led title, useful first 150 description characters,
  chapters for videos over about three minutes
- Shorts: show the operational problem in the first seconds and keep the caption
  under 150 characters where possible
- Never use a result-led clickbait title the video cannot substantiate

## Post structures

### Educational

Specific operational hook -> 2-4 useful points -> what changes in practice ->
question or demo CTA.

### Product demonstration

Situation at the property -> friction in the current handoff -> the ResortPro
workflow shown on screen -> concrete next action.

### Founder/building post

Observed problem -> product decision -> tradeoff or limitation -> lesson ->
invitation for resort operators to respond.

### Customer proof

Verified before state -> action taken -> measured result and timeframe -> named
customer quote/attribution -> relevant CTA.

## CTA library

- Explore the live demo
- See which plan fits your property
- Watch the full workflow
- Tell us how your team handles this today
- Book a ResortPro demo
- Save this checklist for your next busy weekend

Use only one primary CTA in a post.

## Style guardrails

- No “game-changer,” “revolutionary,” “seamless,” “robust,” “leverage,” or
  “streamline your workflow.”
- No “It’s not X. It’s Y.” construction.
- No fake suspense, fake vulnerability, or dramatic em dashes.
- No walls of feature bullets. Select the details that serve the post's idea.
- No unsupported “best,” “fastest,” market-leader, revenue, growth, time-saved,
  uptime, security, or customer-count claims.
- No invented testimonials or founder stories.
- No more than one idea per post unless the format is explicitly a carousel,
  thread, or long-form video.
- Specificity must come from verified product behavior or supplied evidence.

## Approved factual anchors

These can be used after checking the current repository source:

- ResortPro has public Solo, Independent Resort, and Resort Group plans.
- It supports one-property and multi-property operating models, depending on plan.
- The product models bookings, guests, rooms, payments, housekeeping,
  maintenance, inventory, food orders, loyalty, marketing campaigns, and public
  resort content.
- A live role-based demo is available from the marketing site.
- The product experience includes English and Bangla.

Pricing is time-sensitive. Verify `packages/types/src/plans.ts` immediately
before putting a price or plan limit in a post.

## Unverified material

Do not publish the testimonials, resort names, customer logos, or performance
statistics currently displayed in demo/marketing pages unless the founder
confirms they are real, approved, and documented. This includes claims about
check-in time, direct-booking growth, setup time, hours saved, doubled bookings,
hosting, backups, encryption, and uptime.
