# ResortPro 30-day organic social plan

**Campaign window:** 29 October–27 November 2026  
**Campaign name:** One live picture of the day  
**Primary audience:** independent resort owners and general managers in Bangladesh  
**Primary objective:** build category awareness and generate qualified visits to `https://resortpro.site/try`  
**Working target:** 30 attributed live-demo visits during the campaign; revise after the first 7-day baseline  
**Budget assumption:** organic distribution; no paid spend included

## Campaign idea

Running a resort means answering dozens of small operational questions. ResortPro helps the team see bookings, rooms, payments, guests, and service work from shared live information.

### Message hierarchy

1. See the resort day as one connected picture.
2. Improve handoffs without disrespecting the notebooks, calls, and messages that kept the property running.
3. Keep direct booking connected to daily operations.
4. Let the team explore the workflow in the live demo.

### Content mix

- 40% practical hospitality education
- 20% community questions, polls, and operator observations
- 25% product demonstrations and verified capability
- 10% founder/building ResortPro
- 5% direct conversion

## Publishing workflow

1. Approve the core idea and factual claims.
2. Produce one master asset, then adapt it per platform.
3. Add platform-specific caption, CTA, subtitles, and alt text.
4. Preview mobile crops and verify Bangla shaping.
5. Publish or schedule only after final approval.
6. Reply to substantive comments within one working day; turn repeated questions into future posts.

### Timing test

Do not assume a universal “best time.” During week one, test one morning window and one evening window in Bangladesh time for Facebook/Instagram, and one weekday workday window for LinkedIn. Keep the better-performing window for weeks 2–4, based on reach quality, comments, saves, and demo clicks—not impressions alone.

## Metrics and reporting

| Objective | Metric | 30-day working target | Tracking |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Demo interest | Social-attributed visits to `/try` | 30 | UTM links + web analytics |
| Useful content | Saves and meaningful comments | Establish week-one baseline; improve by 15% in weeks 3–4 | Native platform analytics |
| Video quality | Average watch percentage and completion | Improve each week; no unsupported universal benchmark | Reels/Shorts/TikTok analytics |
| Audience growth | Relevant owner/manager followers | Track net qualified growth, not raw count | Profile analytics + manual spot check |
| Conversation | Questions that reveal operational needs | Minimum 10 usable questions | Comment log |

Review on days 7, 15, 23, and 30. Continue formats that produce saves, qualified comments, watch retention, and demo visits; do not repeat a post merely because it collected low-intent views.

## Guardrails

- Do not use unverified customer names, logos, testimonials, or performance statistics.
- Do not claim faster check-in, booking growth, hours saved, uptime, or security standards without evidence.
- Never publish generated screenshots as real product UI.
- Fictional scenarios must be clearly illustrative.
- One post gets one primary CTA.
- When a real customer story becomes available, replace one educational slot rather than adding more volume.

## Dependencies before production

- Confirm which social accounts are active and logged in.
- Confirm whether the founder will appear on camera.
