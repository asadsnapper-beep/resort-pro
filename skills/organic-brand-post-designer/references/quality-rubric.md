# Organic brand-post quality rubric

Score each dimension from 0 to 2. A post should not ship with a zero in any dimension. For a high-stakes campaign, target at least 14/16 and fix the lowest score first.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Message | unclear or competing ideas | understandable after effort | immediate single takeaway |
| Brand specificity | could be any brand | some brand tokens | unmistakable visual grammar and context |
| Human truth | empty aesthetic | relatable but familiar | observed, credible, audience-specific truth |
| Concept | template or decoration | coherent but expected | strong idea expressed through form |
| Hierarchy | no reading order | mostly readable | deliberate first-second-third flow |
| Craft | visible errors or AI artifacts | technically acceptable | refined type, spacing, crop, and detail |
| Accessibility | unreadable or unsafe crop | passes with caveats | readable at thumbnail, good contrast, safe placement |
| Truth and rights | invented/unsupported or unlicensed | unclear provenance | claims verified and assets permitted |

## Anti-generic stress test

Temporarily hide the logo and brand name.

- If nothing still signals the brand, strengthen the visual grammar or contextual evidence.
- If the design depends on a fashionable effect, remove the effect and see whether the idea remains.
- If a stock photo could be swapped with any other without changing the meaning, the image direction is too generic.
- If all elements are perfectly centered, symmetrical, glossy, and equally polished, introduce only the asymmetry or texture that supports the concept.

## Technical inspection

At 100% zoom, check spelling, punctuation, script shaping, logo geometry, face and hand anatomy, shadows, reflections, edge masks, image repetition, and interface accuracy.

At phone-feed size, check focal point, headline readability, contrast, CTA priority, and whether platform chrome or cropping obscures important content.

For carousels, view all slides together and then swipe them in order. Check rhythm, continuity, repeated information, and whether the final slide earns the CTA.
