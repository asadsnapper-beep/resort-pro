# Research basis

This reference records the design evidence that shaped the skill. Re-check time-sensitive platform specifications and emerging trends when using it.

## Findings translated into rules

- Clear hierarchy, spacing, consistency, and readability make social graphics easier to notice and understand; a post should carry one primary message and use the caption for detail.
- Scale, hierarchy, balance, contrast, and Gestalt relationships are foundational. Consistent typography, a refined palette, grid alignment, and whitespace improve usability and perceived quality.
- Brand consistency should be a recognisable visual language, not identical templates. Stable brand decisions free the designer to vary the expressive parts.
- Generic AI output tends toward common patterns and average aesthetics. Human judgment, contextual specificity, unexpected but relevant choices, and deliberate editing are the main differentiators.
- Audiences increasingly notice over-perfect synthetic cues. Natural texture, believable light, real environments, genuine expression, local detail, and selective imperfection can increase credibility when they fit the brand.
- Accessibility remains a design constraint: WCAG calls for at least 4.5:1 contrast for normal text and 3:1 for large text.
