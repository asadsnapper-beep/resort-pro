# ResortPro logo v4

## Final lockup

The primary logo combines the approved geometric **R + small Pro** symbol with
the approved lowercase **resortpro** wordmark. Both text elements use Nunito
ExtraBold (800), while the R remains a custom vector mark.

## Files

- `resortpro-symbol-v4.svg` — R + small Pro symbol for compact brand placements.
- `resortpro-symbol-reversed-v4.svg` — white symbol for dark backgrounds.
- `resortpro-logo-horizontal-v4.svg` — complete primary logo.
- `resortpro-logo-reversed-v4.svg` — complete white logo for dark backgrounds.
- `resortpro-mark-v3.svg` — R-only mark retained for tiny favicon/app-icon use.

## Color

- ResortPro navy: `#183153`
- White: `#FFFFFF`

Keep at least one quarter of the R width as clear space. Do not change the
relative sizes of the small Pro label and the lowercase resortpro wordmark.
