# ResortPro logo v2

## Concept

The mark combines a clear capital **R** with a gold resort roof inside its open
space. The R makes the brand identifiable at favicon size; the roof adds the
hospitality cue without using generic palm trees, beds, or location pins.

## Files

- `resortpro-mark-v2.svg` — standalone icon for favicon, app icon, avatar, and compact headers.
- `resortpro-mark-reversed-v2.svg` — standalone icon for dark backgrounds.
- `resortpro-logo-horizontal-v2.svg` — primary logo on light backgrounds.
- `resortpro-logo-reversed-v2.svg` — white-and-gold lockup for navy or dark backgrounds.

## Colors

- ResortPro navy: `#183153`
- ResortPro gold: `#D4A853`
- White: `#FFFFFF` (reversed lockup only)

## Usage

- Keep clear space around the mark equal to at least one quarter of its width.
- Do not add shadows, gradients, outlines, or a containing shape.
- Do not recolor the gold roof independently.
- Use the standalone mark at 24px or larger on screen.
- Use the horizontal lockup when the available width is at least 140px.
