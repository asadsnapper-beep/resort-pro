# ResortPro logo v3

## Concept

The identity pairs a custom geometric **R** symbol with a lowercase
**resortpro** wordmark set in Nunito ExtraBold. The separated upper and lower
strokes make the mark feel modular and software-led, while the diagonal leg
adds forward motion. There are no hotel, roof, palm, bed, or property symbols.

## Files

- `resortpro-mark-v3.svg` — standalone navy mark for light backgrounds.
- `resortpro-mark-reversed-v3.svg` — standalone white mark for dark backgrounds.
- `resortpro-logo-horizontal-v3.svg` — primary R Pro lockup.
- `resortpro-logo-reversed-v3.svg` — white R Pro lockup for dark backgrounds.

## Typography and color

- Wordmark: Nunito ExtraBold (800)
- ResortPro navy: `#183153`
- White: `#FFFFFF`

## Usage

- Use the full R + resortpro lockup in navigation, marketing, and documents.
- Use the standalone R for favicons, app icons, avatars, and compact UI.
- Keep clear space around the logo equal to at least one quarter of the R width.
- Do not add gradients, outlines, shadows, or hospitality imagery.
- Do not recreate the Pro wordmark in another typeface or weight.
